Billie Eilish

The Life and Music of a Generation-Defining Artist

Early Life and Musical Beginnings

Billie Eilish Pirate Baird O’Connell was born in 2001 in Los Angeles, California. Raised in a creative household and homeschooled, she began writing music with her brother Finneas O’Connell.

Her breakout song Ocean Eyes was uploaded online in 2015 and quickly went viral, launching her career and introducing her haunting pop sound to millions of listeners.

Rise to Fame

Billie Eilish gained worldwide recognition with her debut album When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go? in 2019.

The album featured the hit song bad guy and earned her multiple Grammy Awards including Album of the Year.

Her music blends pop, alternative, and electronic sounds while exploring themes like identity, fame, and mental health.
